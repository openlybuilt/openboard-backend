# Organization chart

- Place the leader at the top and direct reports on the next level. Align siblings and repeat card widths for comparable roles.
- Use a frame for a department or team only when it clarifies ownership. A reporting arrow connects people or roles; containment identifies the department.
- Keep reporting lines distinct from collaboration or communication. Put non-reporting relationships in a separate view if they would blur the hierarchy.
- Check each level, manager, sibling order, and department membership against the brief or reference image.
